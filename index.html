Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Applies to the Kipaji mobile app (Android & iOS)
Published on Google Play as MyKipaji (package com.brianol.kipajimobile) by Worqflow Inc

Kipaji — published on Google Play as MyKipaji by Worqflow Inc — is a mobile tool grassroots cricket coaches in Kenya use to log coaching sessions and submit them for compliance review. This page explains, plainly, what the app collects, why, and who can see it.

01 Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to log a coaching session and route it to your organization for review.

Your account

  • Email address and password, used to sign you in
  • Your name and coaching role, shown to reviewers alongside your submissions
  • The organization (club, school, or federation body) your account belongs to

Session reports you submit

Each compliance session you log includes:

FieldWhat it captures
Session detailsTitle, date range, frequency, how many sessions, the type of activity delivered
Delivery & venueWho delivered the session (you, a teacher, another coach), the receiving organization, venue type, and an optional address or map link
Participant countsTotal attendance, broken down by gender and, optionally, age bracket — counts only, never names
Match/score detailsTeam names and scores, if the session included a scored match
NotesFree-text notes you choose to add
EvidencePhotos, videos, documents, or score sheets you attach as proof the session happened, plus an optional caption

Community and cricket features

FieldWhat it captures
Community messagesThe text of messages you post in the organization channels you belong to, with the time sent
ReportsWhich message you reported and when, so a moderator can review it
BlocksWhich members you have blocked, so their messages are hidden from you
Follows and connectionsPlayers you follow, and connection requests you send or accept
NotificationsWhich in-app notifications you have read
Match scoringScores you record for a match, ball by ball or as a scorecard: the players involved, chosen from your organization's player register, with their runs, wickets and catches

What we don't collect

We don't collect location tracking, contacts, browsing history outside the app, or advertising identifiers. We don't sell your data to anyone, for any reason.

02 How we use it

Session data exists for one purpose: giving your organization an accurate, auditable record that grassroots coaching sessions took place, so they can meet their own compliance and funding-reporting obligations. Your account details let you sign in, and let reviewers know whose submission they're looking at.

Community messages are delivered to the other members of that channel. Reports go to your organization's community moderators, who can remove a message that breaks the community rules. Blocks only change what you see. Follows and connections personalise the players and updates you see. Match scores and scorecards become part of your organization's match records.

03 Who can see it

Your submissions are visible to staff within your own organization who hold a reviewer or administrator role — never to coaches or staff at a different organization, and never to the public. This is enforced at the database level, not just in the app's interface.

Messages you post are visible to the members of that channel and to your organization's administrators. Your reports and blocks are not shown to the member you reported or blocked; reports are visible to you and to your organization's community moderators. Match scores and scorecards are visible to members of your organization.

We do not sell or rent your data to advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

04 Youth participants

Many Kipaji sessions involve young players. The app is built to avoid ever needing their personal information: participant fields capture aggregate counts by age bracket (under-8 through under-17, plus open age), not names, photos of individual children, or any other identifying detail about a minor. Evidence photos are of the session itself, at a coach's discretion, and are not required to include any participant's face. The same applies to the optional caption a coach can add to a piece of evidence — it's free text, so please don't include a child's name or other identifying detail in it.

Kipaji is a tool for adult coaches and administrators. It is not directed at children, and children do not hold accounts or use the app themselves.

Match scoring is the one place a player is named: a score or scorecard records the players in a match, chosen from the player register your organization already keeps, which can include young players. Those records are visible only to members of your organization. The app never asks a coach to type in a new child's details to score a match.

04a Community rules and moderation

Before using community channels, you must agree to the community rules. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive members. You can report any message and block any member from inside the app. Moderators review reports and remove messages that break the rules. Accounts that break the rules lose access.

05 Offline storage on your device

Kenyan grassroots venues don't always have reliable connectivity, so Kipaji is offline-first: everything you enter is saved to a local database on your phone immediately, and synced to our servers automatically once you're back online. Until it syncs, a draft exists only on your device. Your sign-in credentials are stored in your device's secure keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain); other locally-saved data is protected by your device's own screen lock and storage protections, the same as any other app's local data.

06 How long we keep it

Submitted session reports are retained for 3 years after the end of the reporting period they cover, or for as long as your account remains active, whichever is longer, so your organization can meet routine compliance-reporting requirements. If you'd like a submission or your account removed sooner, contact your organization's administrator or us directly (§10).

A message a moderator removes is hidden from everyone straight away. Messages, reports and blocks are kept while your account is active. You can ask for them to be deleted as described in §08.

07 Security

Data in transit between the app and our servers is encrypted (TLS). Access to your organization's data is restricted by row-level database policies keyed to your account and organization — a reviewer at one organization cannot query another's records, even directly against the database. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

08 Your choices

  • Access & correction — ask us or your organization's administrator for a copy of your submitted data, or to correct it.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your account and associated submissions, subject to your organization's own compliance-record obligations.
  • Camera & file access — the app requests camera and file permissions only when you choose to attach evidence, and explains why before the system prompt appears. You can decline and still submit a report without evidence.
